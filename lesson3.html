EXCEL ESSENTIALS / LESSON 03

Let formulas do the work

You don’t need to calculate every line by hand. Write a formula once, then let your spreadsheet update the result.

9 minute lessonLesson 3 of 5Read · Try · Reflect

Learning objective

By the end of this lesson

Multiply cell values, fill a formula down, and use SUM.

Write your first formula

Continue with the unsorted workshop dataset from Lesson 2. In D1, add the heading Line total. In D2, type =B2*C2 and press Enter. Excel multiplies 24 by 4.5 and shows 108.

The equals sign tells Excel to calculate. The asterisk means multiplication. Using cell references means that changing the quantity or unit cost updates the line total automatically.

  1. Select D2 and copy it.
  2. Select D3:D5 and paste. You can also drag D2’s fill handle down to D5.
  3. Select each result and inspect the formula bar. D3 should use =B3*C3.
  4. Format D2:D6 as INR Currency with two decimal places.

Relative references move with the formula

Copying =B2*C2 down one row changes it to =B3*C3. If you later need a fixed reference, dollar signs lock it: $B$2 stays fixed when copied.

Expected calculations before sorting
CellFormulaResult (INR)
D2=B2*C2108.00
D3=B3*C345.00
D4=B4*C424.00
D5=B5*C530.00
D6=SUM(D2:D5)207.00

Add a total with SUM

Type Total in C6. In D6, enter =SUM(D2:D5). SUM adds the numbers in the range. The colon means “from D2 through D5.” You should see ₹207.00.

Keep the total outside the supply records. Including D6 in its own SUM range creates a circular reference, because the formula would depend on itself.

If the result looks wrong, check the formula bar, confirm that the source cells are numbers, and make sure the range includes all four supplies. Parentheses control calculation order: =(B2+2)*C2 adds two notebooks before multiplying.

YOUR TURN

Make it stick

Before sorting, change B2 from 24 to 26. What should D2 and D6 become? Then restore B2 to 24 for the next lesson.

Check your thinking

D2 becomes ₹117.00 (26 × 4.50). D6 becomes ₹216.00. Restoring B2 to 24 returns the line total to ₹108.00 and the overall total to ₹207.00.
