EXCEL ESSENTIALS / LESSON 02

Give your data some structure

Good spreadsheets begin with tidy data. A little structure now makes calculations and charts much easier later.

8 minute lessonLesson 2 of 5Read · Try · Reflect

Learning objective

By the end of this lesson

Enter consistent records and choose helpful number formats.

One row, one record

Our workshop needs four kinds of supplies. Give each supply its own row and each attribute its own column. Put short, unique headings in the first row.

Enter the values in the table below, or download the practice CSV and open it in Excel. A CSV contains plain data; it does not preserve colors, formulas, or multiple worksheets. After opening it, save an .xlsx copy for your work.

  • Keep a single header row. Avoid blank rows inside the dataset.
  • Use the same unit throughout a column. Here, Quantity counts individual items.
  • Store amounts as numbers. Type 4.5 into C2 rather than 4.5 rupees.
  • Avoid merged cells within the dataset; they make sorting and navigation harder.

Format for clarity

Select C2:C5 and apply a Currency number format from the Home tab, choosing the ₹ symbol for Indian rupees (INR). Use two decimal places for consistent prices. The value 4.5 can display as ₹4.50 while remaining a number you can calculate with.

Make the header row bold and widen columns until their contents are readable. Use a subtle fill for headings, with enough contrast between the text and background. Describe meaning in words rather than relying only on color.

Appearance and value are different

Number formatting changes the display, not the underlying value. A percentage is also a number format: 0.25 displays as 25%. Typing 25 and then applying Percentage displays 2500%.

These examples use a decimal point. Depending on your regional settings, Excel may use a decimal comma or show a different currency symbol. Choose the ₹ symbol for Indian rupees (INR) explicitly when practicing.

YOUR TURN

Make it stick

Enter the four records, format the unit costs as INR currency, and make the headings readable. What should C2 display?

Check your thinking

With an Indian rupee (INR) Currency format and two decimal places, C2 displays ₹4.50. Its numeric value remains 4.5. There should be one header row and four data rows.
